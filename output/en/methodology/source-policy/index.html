Source Policy

How TourVsTravel selects, evaluates, and cites information used in travel experience comparisons.

Sourcing standards

All factual claims used in comparisons draw from primary sources: official tourism authority data, peer-reviewed academic research, and publicly documented operator specifications. We do not accept sponsored placements or affiliate arrangements that influence content.

Source types

Accepted source categories include government tourism statistics, published academic studies on travel behavior, certified operator specifications, and independently verified traveler accounts. Unverifiable anecdotal sources are excluded.
